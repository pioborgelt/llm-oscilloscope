# Moving Both Detector Channels To Qwen
The first transfer results released here used Qwen to process continuations generated by Llama. This test is significantly harder, as Qwen generated its own answers and the same unlabeled adapter had to carry both detector channels onto the new token stream of Qwen.

I fixed the adapter before Qwen generated the evaluation set, and it was fitted on paired internal states without using Qwen entity or support labels. An external grader then aligned the answer entities after generation.

## What was done
Qwen generated 2,230 tokens for 400 questions from the SimpleQA and GRANOLA
datasets that do not occur in the original detector data. The grader aligned
464 answer endpoints overall. Of these, 442 were support-scorable: 87 supported
and 355 unsupported. The remaining 10 unverifiable and 12 partial endpoints
were excluded from the support evaluation.

Transported entity completion channel: 0.954 AUROC / 0.824 AP

Transported support channel: 0.717 AUROC / 0.903 AP

Randomly paired adapters reach 0.471 and 0.511 AUROC.

The secondary product of both readings reaches **0.912 AUROC / 0.574 AP** on
unsupported endpoints in the generated token stream. It was not used to pass
the frozen primary test.

## Limitations

The support transfer is uneven. It reaches 0.742 AUROC on GRANOLA but only 0.509 on SimpleQA, where there are just 12 supported and 205 unsupported endpoints. Its high AP also reflects that 80.3% of the endpoints are unsupported. The frozen criteria pass, but this is not yet a reliable cross-model support detector.

What the result does show is that one adapter fitted without target-model annotations can preserve useful information from two different measurement channels on Qwen's own generations. This is short factual QA on two related open models, not long-form or architecture-general validation.

The compact predictions, portable adapter and frozen result are in `artifacts/qwen_native_transfer/`. `TransportedQwenDetector` applies the two readings to Qwen L23 post-token states. The main verifier recomputes the metrics, controls and decision.
